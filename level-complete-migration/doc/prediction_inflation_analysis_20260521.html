Root Cause #1 — Architecture Over-Generalization (5–13× prediction bias confirmed): Cross-dataset label analysis confirms the UL PSN head predicts 5–13× above its own training label rates for most high-ratio combos. This is not a label data problem — UL training labels are comparable to legacy for the majority of events. The model architecture over-generalizes from correlated game-level and CTX features (shared target_game_id embedding) rather than learning event-specific signal. Even LEVEL_END_SUCCESS combos with zero positive labels in training receive 61–95% predictions. This is the dominant driver of over-bidding and requires architecture-level or calibration fixes.
Root Cause #2 — Missing Product Accuracy Calibration: The _calib_tensor in UL DeployModel is all-ones until refresh_product_accuracy_calibration.py runs against live traffic and the model is redeployed. Legacy BHV applies per-campaign evt_rate_factor correction. Once populated, the calibration tensor can correct architecture bias by up to 20× (clamped to [0.05, 1.0]) — this is the most actionable near-term fix.
Root Cause #3 — LEVEL_END_SUCCESS & Sparse Events: Zero Labels, Non-Zero Predictions: Games like 500230137 (LES_40), 500182711 (LES_20), 500205921 (LES_50) have zero positive labels in the UL training sample yet the model predicts 61–79%. FTD-family events average 6.5× over-prediction for the same reason. The shared target_game_id embedding bleeds signal from wildcard * campaigns into specific-event predictions. Mechanism 1 (eligibility gate) must zero these out at serving time — it is the critical safety net until calibration is fully operational.
Root Cause #4 — Label Inflation Isolated to tlevelplayed (1.5–1.6×): Unlike other event types, tlevelplayed shows genuine UL training label inflation of 1.5–1.6× vs legacy (games 500247636, 500259204). This is consistent with wildcard * campaign contamination of the tlevelplayed embedding in Stage 2 collect_set aggregation. However, even this inflation is insufficient to explain 3×+ over-prediction — architecture bias accounts for the remainder. For all other event groups, UL and legacy label rates are broadly comparable (label inflation ≈ 1.0×).

But even this inflation is insufficient to explain 3x+ over-prediction — architecture bias accounts for the rest.
