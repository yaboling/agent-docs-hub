Live Traffic Confirms Improvement: The model is performing even better on live 10% traffic than in shadow mode. Median ratio vs BHV is 0.96x (shadow was 1.04x) and vs CTX is 0.95x — both near-perfect parity. At 3.9% of combos ≥2x over-bidding vs BHV (down from 43% pre-gating, 8.7% in shadow), the gating and calibration mechanisms are working correctly in production.
High-Ratio Outliers — Mostly Sparse Legacy Data: The top outlier is build_3manor on game 500023189 at 11.15x — but legacy has only 6 starts vs 42,227 UL starts. Similarly star5_hero_received (500186905, 4.92x) has 36 legacy starts vs 20,323 UL starts. These are artifacts of sparse legacy traffic, not genuine model over-prediction. The only concerning case with substantial legacy volume is log_city_upgrade_6 (500101393, 2.6x, 13,268 legacy starts & 33,640 UL starts) — worth investigating.
GRT Events Still Under-Bidding: GRT events show median 0.71x vs BHV (consistent with shadow analysis at 0.72x). This systematic under-bidding pattern is stable and may reduce impression volume on GRT campaigns. Review calibration factors for GRT event types if revenue recovery is a priority.
Increased Combo Coverage (138 → 356 vs BHV): Live traffic covers 2.6x more campaign/game/event combos than the shadow snapshot, giving much higher confidence in the per-group statistics. LEVEL_END_SUCCESS now has 36 combos (vs ~9 in shadow), tlevelplayed has 49 combos, GRT has 21 combos.
