Major Improvement Confirmed: Gating + calibration has dramatically reduced over-bidding. The median UL/Legacy ratio dropped from 1.77x → 1.04x (near-perfect parity). The share of combos ≥2x over-bidding fell from 43% → 9%, and ≥3x from 17% → 3%. Additionally, ~90 previously over-bidding combos are now gated out entirely (no UL traffic), preventing excess spend on those campaigns.
Remaining Over-Bidding (4 combos ≥3x): af_ftd on game 500177040 remains the highest ratio at 8.42x — but this is a single UL start vs 2069 legacy starts, so the UL prediction is likely a one-off sample artifact rather than systematic over-bidding. star5_hero_received (500186905, 3.85x, 458 starts) and pc_t2_d60_ios_custom (500042705, 3.06x, 334 starts) warrant closer monitoring. Absolute prediction values are low for these events.
Increased Under-Bidding (43% of combos <1x): The calibration appears to have over-corrected on some event groups, particularly GRT events (median 0.72x) and LEVEL_END_SUCCESS (median 0.80x). These combos are now under-bidding vs legacy. This is a safer failure mode than over-bidding, but may reduce impression volume on those campaigns. Review calibration factors for GRT and LES event types to prevent revenue loss.
Note on Reduced Combo Count (228 → 138): The eligibility gate has zeroed out predictions for ~90 combos that previously showed significant over-bidding (including most LEVEL_END_SUCCESS sparse events and FTD combos). This is the intended behavior of Mechanism 1. Those combos no longer appear in traffic data, confirming the gate is functional.
