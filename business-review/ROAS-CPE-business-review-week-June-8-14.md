## Key Themes

1. **Portfolio-wide spend growth is healthy, but revenue quality is softening.** Total spend grew +5.17% to $56.56M with revenue up +4.78%. The gap is small but consistent across d28 adRevenue, d0, and CPE — suggesting cheaper installs are being acquired at scale without commensurate monetization uplift.

2. **d7 IAP is the clear ROAS star.** Installs +14.99%, revenue +9.55% outpacing spend +9.21%, margin improved +0.13pp to 42.34%, MMP eCPI declining -5.13%. All signals positive — highest-quality and highest-margin ROAS segment with clear room to scale.

3. **d28 adRevenue margin is eroding fastest in ROAS.** Spend +13.70%, installs +20.75%, but revenue only +10.68% — a 3pp gap driving -0.80pp margin compression to 30.76%. The lowest-margin ROAS segment is growing volume but acquiring lower-value users.

4. **CPE Purchase is the standout across all campaign types.** Revenue +21.58% outpacing spend +18.70%, margin improving +0.76pp, installs +24.03%. This segment should receive priority budget allocation.

5. **CPE Retention is a 🚨 critical issue.** A -3.92pp single-week margin collapse with spend +8.56% and revenue -2.36% is a serious quality signal. MMP eCPI +11.11% points to post-install event degradation. Escalate to segment owners for immediate advertiser-level review.

6. **CPE Level Complete is contracting in quality and volume.** Spend -11.92%, revenue -13.95%, margin -0.94pp — all trending negatively. The segment may be losing its higher-quality advertisers to competitive pressure or seasonal patterns.

7. **d7 adRevenue install volume is declining despite flat spend.** Installs -5.56%, starts -4.18% with dCPI +4.85% and MMP eCPI +7.23%. Auction cost inflation is real in this segment and needs monitoring to determine whether it stabilizes or accelerates.
