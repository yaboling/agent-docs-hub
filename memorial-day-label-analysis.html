Memorial Day vs Prior 2-Week Baseline (May 11–24)

Executive Summary: IAP Rate shows a consistent +5–6% uplift on Memorial Day (D1 and D3). While the AdRev Rate and Retention Rate remained flat, the average AdRev amount increased by 8.0–8.9%. This indicates that holiday leisure drives longer, higher-value sessions among engaged users rather than changing the baseline ad-engagement rate.

Model Impact & Action: These label shifts reflect real, recurring seasonal user behavior — not data corruption or pipeline errors. The model is expected to naturally absorb and generalize this pattern over time as Memorial Day cohorts accumulate in training data. No data filtering, blocking, or manual intervention is needed. The corrupted row on May 10 (game 500227161, $8.74M IAP) is a separate issue documented in the anomaly investigation. Recommended action: closely monitor post-Memorial Day cohort performance in the next training cycle to confirm the model correctly weights the seasonal signal without overfitting to it.

May 10 Anomaly: avg_iap_d7 Spike ($95.6)

Unrelated to Memorial Day. On 2026-05-10, avg IAP amount per payer (d1 and d7) jumped to ~$95 vs normal $18-30. IAP rate was completely normal (1.04%), so the number of payers wasn't unusual — those who paid spent 3-4x more. Possible causes: high-value game promotion, limited-time bundle, or data pipeline issue. Recommend cross-checking by target_game_id for that install cohort.
